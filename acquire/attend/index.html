07 — FAQ

Common
Questions

Everything you need before you apply.

What experience level is ASCENSION designed for?

No prior retreat experience required. ASCENSION is designed for people who are serious about transformation — not for a specific level of yoga practice or prior wellness familiarity. We assess readiness through the application, not credentials. If you are ready to show up fully for seven days, you are ready.

What does "all-inclusive" actually include?

The edition covers all group modalities and workshops, private fascial release sessions (2 for 7-day / 5 for 14-day), 4–5 Hideout Wellness thermal recovery sessions, Sleep Science Conference access and personal sleep assessment, daily breakfast, lunch and dinner plus non-alcoholic beverages, and airport transfers on arrival and departure day. Accommodation, international flights, travel insurance, and personal spending are not included — attendees book their own hotel from the curated list, with group rates and booking codes provided.

What is fascial release and why does it matter?

Fascia is the connective tissue that surrounds every cell, muscle, and organ in your body — and it is also where emotional stress is stored. Myofascial release techniques reshape fascia at the molecular level, releasing adhesions that hold emotional weight. After a session: better posture, freer movement, and an emotional ease that most modalities cannot produce on their own.

Why Da Nang, Vietnam?

Da Nang is one of the most underrated luxury wellness destinations in the world — warm year-round, directly on the South China Sea, with world-class resort infrastructure and a rich tradition of Vietnamese healing practices. January is ideal: dry season, 28–30°C, low tourist traffic. And Vietnamese practitioners bring modalities that Western retreat circuits rarely access at a serious level.

How many people will be at the retreat?

ASCENSION is limited to a founding cohort of wellness professionals, influencers, content creators, practitioners, and media. This is deliberate — every session feels personal, every practitioner knows your name, and the group energy stays coherent rather than diluted. Applications are reviewed individually for fit, readiness, and contribution to the cohort.

Can I attend the 14-day Fortnight or do I have to decide upfront?

You can reserve either the 7-Day Edition or the full 14-Day Fortnight at booking. The 14-day option includes the Tết Rituals day and the Closing Ceremony & Tết Blessing. If you book the 7-day and wish to extend, we will accommodate subject to availability — but the Fortnight rate cannot be guaranteed after arrival. We recommend deciding before you arrive.

I am a practitioner. How do I apply to facilitate?

Use the Facilitate section below, or visit the dedicated Facilitator Portal. Tell us your modality, how long you have been practicing, who you have worked with, and what you would bring to an ASCENSION cohort. Vietnamese practitioners with local knowledge and international training are especially welcome. We review applications on a rolling basis.
