Selection Criteria

The MODUS
Curation Standard

Every ASCENSION partner is evaluated against 8 criteria. We don't accept every hotel, brand, or practitioner. Curation is the product.

01
Quality
Does the experience deliver authentic transformation or tangible value?
02
Aesthetic
Does it fit the MODUS world? Design, atmosphere, visual coherence.
03
Authenticity
Is there substance behind the brand or service? Real expertise, not hype.
04
People
Who delivers this? Background, training, and integrity.
05
Place
Does it have a meaningful relationship with Da Nang and the destination?
06
Sustainability
Is the operation responsible? Environmentally, socially, ethically?
07
Story
Can it contribute to the larger ASCENSION narrative?
08
Guest Value
Would we personally recommend it? Would we attend ourselves?

This standard certifies that every ASCENSION partner has been thoughtfully selected to serve our attendees' transformation.
