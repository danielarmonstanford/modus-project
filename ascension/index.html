The Moment

Prepare for
the New Year.

Tết Nguyên Đán — the Lunar New Year — is the most auspicious day in the Vietnamese calendar. The Year of the Fire Goat begins January 29, 2027. ASCENSION ends January 26. You will arrive at that threshold already transformed.

The 14-Day Fortnight includes a full Tết preparation day on January 24: ancestor veneration, bánh chưng making, calligraphy, red envelope ritual, and fire ceremony. Then you step into the New Year with intention.
