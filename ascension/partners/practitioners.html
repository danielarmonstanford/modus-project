Practitioner
Terms

Compensation model, referral structure, accommodation terms, sponsored-training benefits, and editorial rights for ASCENSION practitioner collaborators. January 12–26, 2027 · Da Nang, Vietnam.

Philosophy

Mastery
is the standard.

MODUS selects practitioners by reputation, specificity of mastery, and congruence with the ASCENSION arc. There are no generalists in this programme. Each practitioner is the best available for their modality — and is treated accordingly: full accommodation, visible attribution, and fair compensation.

The model is designed so that practitioners benefit beyond the immediate engagement: editorial exposure, referral income, and the credibility of being a MODUS Séance collaborator. This is a long-term relationship, not a one-time contract.

Compensation Model

How practitioners
are paid.

Practitioner compensation is structured across three components: a session fee, a referral percentage, and an optional sponsored-training credit. Per-practitioner negotiated amounts are agreed directly with MODUS and are not published in this portal. The structure below applies to all practitioners.

Session Fee

Flat per-session fee

Agreed pre-retreat. Paid within 14 days of retreat close. Covers both group sessions and any assigned private sessions.

Referral Fee

10% of referred passport

For each confirmed attendee who lists the practitioner as their referral source on application. Paid with session fee after retreat.

Accommodation

Covered by MODUS

Full accommodation for the duration of your programme engagement, at a curated property from the stay menu. Category agreed on LOI.

Sponsored Training

Optional credit

Select practitioners may participate in a cross-training day with another modality. MODUS covers the cost. Available to all practitioners who opt in.

Per-practitioner session fee amounts are agreed directly and confidentially. This portal publishes structure only.

General Terms
  • Exclusivity: Practitioners do not facilitate competing retreats in Da Nang within the programme window (Jan 12–26) without MODUS consent.
  • Attribution: All MODUS editorial content featuring the retreat credits practitioners by full name and modality. Practitioners may use this credit in their own marketing materials.
  • Editorial rights: MODUS retains editorial rights to retreat photography and video. Practitioners receive a curated content package (10–20 assets) for personal use within 30 days of retreat close.
  • Intellectual property: Session content, protocols, and teaching methodologies remain the intellectual property of the practitioner. MODUS may describe the session in editorial copy with practitioner approval.
  • Cancellation: Withdrawal by either party up to 90 days before the retreat (October 14, 2026) carries no penalty. Within 90 days, a kill fee of 25% of agreed session fee applies.
  • Conduct: Practitioners are expected to maintain the tone and standards of the ASCENSION experience. MODUS has the right to remove a practitioner from the programme if standards are not upheld, with session fee pro-rated to sessions delivered.
  • Travel: International flights are the responsibility of the practitioner. MODUS may contribute a travel stipend in some cases — this is agreed individually on LOI and not a standard term.
Sponsored Training

Learn from
each other.

ASCENSION practitioners are invited to observe and participate in one modality outside their own, facilitated by a fellow practitioner. MODUS covers the cost. This is a voluntary programme designed to generate cross-disciplinary insight and build genuine relationships between the cohort's facilitators.

Opt-in at LOI or during the programme. Pairings are suggested by MODUS based on modality complementarity — final pairings confirmed with both practitioners.
