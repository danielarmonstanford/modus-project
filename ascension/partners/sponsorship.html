Sponsorship
Tiers

Three sponsorship tiers with defined deliverables. All tiers represent an authentic alignment with the ASCENSION audience — high-performance individuals investing in transformation. January 12–26, 2027 · Da Nang, Vietnam.

Philosophy

Integration,
not interruption.

ASCENSION does not run traditional sponsorship activations. Every brand present is editorially chosen by MODUS as congruent with the retreat's values: mastery, longevity, intentional living. Sponsorship is a collaboration, not a placement. Deliverables are co-created with MODUS to feel native to the experience.

Per the Critical Content Rule: per-partner negotiated numbers and bespoke commercial terms are discussed directly and are not published here. What follows is the tier structure and standard deliverable set.
