2018

Museum of Image and Sound (MIS), São Paulo

Solo Exhibition Invitation

Following an in-person meeting in São Paulo, Cleber Papa, Cultural Director of MIS, reviewed Stanford's portfolio — including Brazil: Sea of Dunes (2014) and the FASCINASIA mixed-media series — and extended an invitation for a solo exhibition, proposing the museum's second-floor gallery space. Production discussions including wall allocation and shipping logistics were underway in late 2019 before being paused by the COVID-19 pandemic. The invitation remains open.

2014 – 2018

Macaya Gallery & Art Basel Miami

Curator · Exhibiting Artist · Solo Show · Wynwood Art District, Miami

Stanford held a sustained presence at Macaya Gallery, Wynwood Art District, Miami, across multiple Art Basel Miami and Miami Art Week editions, both as an exhibiting artist and as curator of group exhibitions.

DÉCOVER Magazine 2nd Anniversary — Galerie du Plateau, Montréal

Group Exhibition

Editorial Features

KA Magazine — Editorial Features

KA Loves Daniel Stanford Art · A True Heart-ist

Two dedicated editorial profiles by KA Magazine exploring Stanford's body of work and confirming two significant additional credentials: acceptance into Verge Art Fair Miami (held concurrent with Art Basel Miami at The Essex House and Clevelander Hotels), and a commission by the Lise Watier Foundation to produce Golden Lionesses, a 36×74 inch triptych unveiled at the Foundation's Black Tie fundraising gala — the painting became the Foundation's iconic symbol for inspiring underprivileged women.
