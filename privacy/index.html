Effective November 19, 2026

MODUS is published by Stanford Emporium Inc. This policy explains how we handle information submitted through the MODUS Join list and MODUS Private waitlist.

Information we collect

When you join a list, we collect your email address, the list you selected, and the date and time of your consent. We also use limited technical information to prevent spam and excessive submissions.

How we use it

We use your information to send the editorial, membership, or launch communications you requested; maintain the subscriber record; prevent abuse; and measure the reliability of our signup systems. We do not sell subscriber information.

Storage and service providers

Subscriber records are stored in access-controlled infrastructure used by MODUS. Email delivery, operational notifications, and optional customer relationship tools may process the information on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.

Your choice

You may unsubscribe using the link in a marketing email or request access, correction, or deletion by contacting editorial@modus.gallery.

Retention and security

We retain subscriber information while your consent remains active or as required for legal and operational records. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.
