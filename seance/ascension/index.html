Why ASCENSION Exists

For more than
two decades,
I've been searching.

Across continents, cultures, and different approaches to movement, recovery, bodywork, meditation, and wellbeing, I've encountered practitioners and practices that changed the way I understood feeling well.

ASCENSION grew from that journey — a chance to bring together some of the people, practices, and discoveries worth experiencing, and place them within one extraordinary destination.

Not a prescription.
Not a promise.
An invitation to explore what feeling better might mean for you.

Daniel Stanford
Founder / Curator, MODUS
