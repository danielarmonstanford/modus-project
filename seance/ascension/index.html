Da Nang, Vietnam · Venue Selection

Three Venues
Under Review

Da Nang offers exceptional luxury wellness infrastructure. We are evaluating three properties against the same criteria: practitioner access, group space, proximity to beach, and the ability to hold a high-intent container without compromise.

Final venue will be announced with the first speaker reveal in Fall 2026. All three options provide the baseline required — what differentiates them is the experience architecture.

Front Runner Option A

Fusion Maia Resort

★★★★★

  • All-inclusive wellness resort — spa access built in
  • Dedicated spa villas, strong for private sessions
  • Pool villa format creates natural retreat atmosphere
  • Strong wellness infrastructure & partnership history
  • Direct beach access, intimate group size suited
  • Smaller conference capacity — fits our group size well, limited expansion
  • Premium price point passes through to attendees

Best fit for immersive container & private session architecture

Option B

Sheraton Grand Da Nang

★★★★★

  • Large conference facilities — ideal for Sleep Science panel
  • Established international brand, easy for global attendees
  • Beachfront location, extensive room inventory
  • Strong F&B infrastructure for ceremony dinners
  • Hotel format — less intimate than resort
  • Wellness facilities require supplemental arrangement
  • Larger property can dilute retreat atmosphere

Best fit for larger group capacity & conference-first format

Option C

TIA Wellness Resort

★★★★★

  • Purpose-built wellness resort — deepest spa integration
  • Strong yoga and movement studio infrastructure
  • Curated wellness programming already in place
  • Quiet, dedicated atmosphere — minimal external noise
  • Conference space more limited than Sheraton
  • Less known internationally — logistics for global attendees
  • Smaller property constrains scaling if demand grows

Best fit for wellness-first experience & deepest integration

The Beaches

My Son Beach · Non Nuoc · Cua Dai · Marble Mountains

Da Nang's coastline is sculptural — limestone cliffs rising from golden sand, year-round warmth, warm waters that invite you in rather than resist you. The beaches here aren't Instagram traps. They're working retreats: places where you run at sunrise, where your mind settles into rhythm, where the ocean becomes your meditation teacher.

January 2027 is perfect: 25°C days, clear skies, gentle swells. The kind of weather that doesn't distract you from transformation. It just holds you.

The People

Vietnam rebuilt itself twice. Once economically. Once spiritually.

Walk into Da Nang and you feel it — the quietness of a place that has survived, adapted, and chosen grace. The hospitality isn't performative. It's real. Practitioners here carry lineages of healing that run back centuries: acupressure, herbal medicine, Vietnamese massage as science, not spa theater.

Meet your facilitators. You'll find Vietnam's best: teachers who train internationally but root themselves here, who understand transformation from the inside.

The Food

Phở that tastes like intention. Fresh herbs that grow in the gardens you walk through. Seafood so clean it tastes like ocean.

Da Nang's food culture is functional luxury — nourishment designed by people who understand that what you eat shapes your transformation. No processed retreat meals. Real food. Local food. The kind that rebuilds you.
