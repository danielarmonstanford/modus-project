Lead Feature · MODUS Terrain

Résidences Boutique, Mont-Tremblant

Québec · Laurentians · Canada

There are invitations that signal something beyond logistics. When Redbox Media extended an invitation to experience Résidences Boutique at Mont-Tremblant, it was less a property tour than a deliberate orchestration — a curator's gesture introducing a client to a body of work. The reception itself became part of the design argument.

The gated community announces itself with restraint. Security and exclusivity are handled as infrastructure rather than theater. You pass through nothing that performs; everything that functions. By the time the chalet emerges from the forest density, you're already inside the design logic.

What strikes immediately is the coherence. Modern touches — clean lines, material simplicity, refined fixtures — rest entirely naturally within a structure that remains fundamentally connected to its forest context. This is not nature dressed up in contemporary clothing. It's contemporary design that understands it's in conversation with landscape.

Feature · MODUS Terrain

Amanwella, Tangalle

Sri Lanka · South Coast · Indian Ocean

Thirty private suites face the Indian Ocean. No children under twelve. No itinerary unless you ask for one. The property sits on a headland above a crescent of white sand that has no name on most maps — the kind of beach that makes you understand why people stopped looking.

Amanwella's architecture belongs to the land rather than the period. Dutch colonial references light enough to read as restraint rather than pastiche. Thatch, laterite, and open air — the materials of the place, arranged with the precision that is the Aman signature. The pool runs along the cliff edge and appears, from certain angles, to continue into the sea below.

The spa operates on the principle that silence is the treatment. Ayurvedic traditions without the performance of wellness. The food is simple in the way good ingredients require: a fish caught that morning, a coconut milk that tastes of the tree it came from.
